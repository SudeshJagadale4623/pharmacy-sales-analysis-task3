## Task 1: Healthcare Data Cleaning & Understanding

Cleaned and analyzed a healthcare patient dataset containing age, gender,
disease, medication, and dosage information. Identified and fixed missing
values, duplicate records, and inconsistent formatting, then performed
basic analysis (patient count, common diseases, average age).

**Files:**
- `raw_healthcare_data.csv` — original (messy) dataset
- `data_cleaning_analysis.py` — cleaning & analysis script
- `cleaned_healthcare_data.csv` — final cleaned dataset
- `analysis_summary.txt` — analysis output
- `Project_Report.md` — full write-up

**Key Findings:** 120 unique patients, average age 51.8 years, Diabetes
was the most common condition (28 patients).

## Task 3: Pharmacy Sales & Drug Analysis

Analyzed pharmacy sales data to identify top-selling medicines, monthly
sales growth, and compared branded vs. generic medicine performance.

**Files:**
- `pharmacy_sales_data.csv` — sales dataset (1,245 orders)
- `sales_analysis.py` — analysis script
- `pharmacy_analysis_summary.txt` — analysis output
- `monthly_sales_trend.png` — monthly sales chart
- `top_medicines_revenue.png` — top 10 medicines by revenue
- `branded_vs_generic.png` — branded vs generic sales share
- `Pharmacy_Project_Report.md` — full write-up

**Key Findings:** $33,025.50 total sales across 1,245 orders. Branded
medicines drove more total revenue despite fewer orders; generics sold
in higher quantity.
